# Ice Cold Beer

A Unity remake of the classic mechanical arcade game.

## About the Original Game

**Ice Cold Beer** is a legendary mechanical arcade game released by Taito in 1983. Unlike typical video arcade games of its era, Ice Cold Beer featured a unique electromechanical design that set it apart in arcades worldwide.

### Original Gameplay

The original game featured a vertical wooden playfield mounted in an arcade cabinet where a video screen would normally be. The playfield was dotted with holes of varying point values, and players controlled a steel ball bearing that rested on a metal bar. The bar could be tilted left and right using two control knobs, requiring careful coordination to navigate the ball through the maze of holes.

The objective was to guide the ball into specific lit holes while avoiding the many other holes on the playfield. As players progressed, the holes would become more challenging to reach, requiring precise control and steady hands. The game's purely mechanical nature - with no computer graphics or digital displays - gave it a tactile, physical quality that made it instantly engaging and highly addictive.

### Historical Significance

Ice Cold Beer became a cult classic in the arcade scene, beloved for its simple yet challenging gameplay and its unique mechanical design. The game's straightforward concept - tilting a bar to guide a ball into holes - proved timeless, and original machines are now highly sought after by collectors.

The game was also released under alternate names in different markets, including "Saloon" and other variations, but "Ice Cold Beer" remains the most iconic version.

## This Remake

This project is a faithful digital recreation of the classic Ice Cold Beer arcade game, built in Unity. It aims to capture the physics-based gameplay and challenge of the original mechanical game while making it accessible to modern players.
